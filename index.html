Three systems I built and ran

ClinicFlow

OYA · live in production
Problem
Clinics run their bookings through WhatsApp. The front desk answers the same questions all day, messages get missed after hours, and patients don't show up.
What I built
Co-founded OYA with two technical partners and built ClinicFlow: an AI agent on the official WhatsApp API that books, reschedules and reminds. I own the commercial side — branding, lead generation, outreach, funnels and CRM, and closing. Stack: Next.js, n8n, Supabase/PostgreSQL, OpenAI, Meta WhatsApp Cloud API.
Result
Live with three clients, including a paying retainer. One month at one clinic:
55bookings made by the agent
1,178inbound patient messages handled
563automated reminders sent

One clinic, one month of production data.

Alfa Fruits outreach engine

Fresh-produce export
Problem
Finding and reaching procurement managers at Egyptian exporters was manual, slow, and died whenever nobody had time to follow up.
What I built
An n8n pipeline: automated lead collection through Serper.dev, a multi-step outreach sequence over the official WhatsApp Business API with email fallback, and an AI reply handler.
Result
Prospecting ran without me. The handler passed a human only the conversations worth having, with a summary of the chat attached. Site: alfa-fruits.com
